\section*{LLM Usage Disclosure}
This work was developed with the assistance of large language models (LLMs) as writing and
analysis tools. LLMs were used to assist with drafting and editing portions of the manuscript
text and with generating portions of the reproducibility documentation. All scientific claims,
numerical results, statistical analyses, figure generation, and conclusions were produced and
verified by the authors through committed scripts and data artifacts; LLM-assisted output was
reviewed, corrected, and validated by the authors. No LLM was used to generate or fabricate
experimental results.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
This study uses publicly available, de-identified datasets: the GDSC2 cell-line drug-response
panel and public TCGA-BRCA data. No human subjects, clinical trials, or protected health
information were involved, and cell-line analyses are not subject to human-subjects review. All
quantum results are exact statevector simulations; no quantum hardware was used. The work reports
a negative methodological result intended to improve reporting standards in quantum machine
learning for biology; it informs no clinical decisions.

\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
All experiments were run from committed scripts in the accompanying repository. Every figure is
generated from committed JSON result artifacts via the scripts referenced in the Reproduction
section; numeric claims in Methods and Results carry provenance to committed scripts. Quantum
results are exact statevector simulations (Qiskit, 12 qubits, ZZFeatureMap fidelity kernel);
there is no QPU execution and no stochastic shot noise, so results are deterministic given the
seed. Reproduction commands are listed in the Reproduction section of the paper.
